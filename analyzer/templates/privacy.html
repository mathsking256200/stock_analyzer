Privacy Policy

Stock Analyzer respects the privacy of its users. This privacy policy explains how user information is collected, used, and protected when using the platform.

Eligibility Criteria

Age of users must be 18 or more, then only they are eligible to fill the registration form and use the platform.

Registration Information

When users register on the platform, basic information such as name, email address, password and so on, can be collected. This information is used only for account creation and user authentication.

Login Security

User login credentials are kept secure and are used only to verify authorized access to the platform. We do not share login information with third parties.

Password Reset

If users forget their password, they can reset it through the password recovery feature. This process ensures that only the authorized account owner can reset their password.

Payment Information

For Registration of the platform may require payment of thousand rupees once. Payment processing is handled securely through Razorpay and sensitive financial information is not stored directly within the application.

Data Protection

We take appropriate security measures to protect user data from unauthorized access, misuse or disclosure.

Changes to Policy

This privacy policy may be updated occasionally to reflect improvements or changes in the platform.
